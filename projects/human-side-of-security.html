PROJECT 01 / HUMAN-CENTERED GRC

The Human Side of Security

Human-centered IAM and GRC program for a fictional logistics environment.

The Problem

Peachtree Logistics Group, a fictional regional logistics company, has inconsistent IAM governance across cloud, on-premises, warehouse, mobile, and third-party environments. Risks include account compromise, excessive access, offboarding gaps, mobile exposure, payment fraud, vendor access, and operational disruption.

My Approach

I treated the business operation as part of the security problem. The project moves from context, assets, data flows, and risk into RBAC, IAM lifecycle procedures, control testing, evidence, metrics, incident scenarios, and human-factors analysis.

What I Built

Fifteen core deliverables spanning business context, risk, control design, IAM procedures, testing, metrics, human factors, and executive reporting; a 90-day roadmap; and controls designed around frontline workflows, mobile work, couriers, vendors, and resilience.

Methods: NIST CSF 2.0 · NIST SP 800-53 · IAM · RBAC · GRC
