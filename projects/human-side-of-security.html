PROJECT 01 / HUMAN-CENTERED GRC

The Human Side of Security

A human-centered IAM and GRC program designed around the realities of a fictional 225-person logistics environment.

The operating challenge

Peachtree Logistics Group is a fictional logistics organization spanning Microsoft 365, SaaS, on-premises systems, warehouse operations, mobile devices, contractors, third parties, and sensitive delivery information. The security problem is not simply selecting controls; it is making access, accountability, evidence, and response work across a fast-moving operating environment.

How I approached it

I started with business context, assets, data flows, stakeholders, and risk. From there, I translated the risk picture into RBAC and IAM lifecycle controls, awareness, incident response, control testing, metrics, evidence expectations, and human-factor considerations. The design keeps the security objective intact while accounting for the people expected to execute it.

The goal is not documentation for its own sake. The goal is traceable security work with clear ownership, practical execution, and evidence that supports review and improvement.

What the work demonstrates

The program includes 15 core deliverables and a 90-day roadmap. Together they show how risk assessment, IAM governance, control design, testing, KPIs/KRIs, incident response, third-party considerations, and executive reporting can operate as one connected program rather than separate documents.
