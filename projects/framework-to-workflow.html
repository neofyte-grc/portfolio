PROJECT 03 / GRC ENGINEERING

From Framework to Workflow

An IAM operating model that translates governance requirements into accountable, measurable, evidence-producing workflows.

The operating challenge

Policies and control statements do not execute themselves. In the fictional environment, access requests, approvals, provisioning, reviews, revocations, exceptions, and evidence collection can vary across systems and departments unless the operating model makes ownership and sequence explicit.

How I approached it

I built a traceability chain from business requirement and risk through framework requirement, control objective, workflow, owner, evidence, test, metric, exception, and improvement. That structure forces each control to answer practical questions about who acts, what triggers the work, how completion is demonstrated, and what happens when the normal path breaks.

The goal is not documentation for its own sake. The goal is traceable security work with clear ownership, practical execution, and evidence that supports review and improvement.

What the work demonstrates

Seven connected IAM workflows cover joiner-mover-leaver, access requests, provisioning, periodic reviews, privileged access, emergency revocation, and exceptions. Policies, standards, templates, fictional evidence, diagrams, testing, metrics, monitoring, and remediation planning support those workflows.
