PROJECT 03 / GRC ENGINEERING

From Framework to Workflow

An IAM operating model that translates governance requirements into accountable, measurable, evidence-producing workflows.

The Problem

The fictional organization has policies and informal access practices, but requests, approvals, provisioning, reviews, revocations, exceptions, and evidence collection vary across systems and departments.

My Approach

I built traceability from business requirement and risk through framework requirement, control objective, workflow, owner, evidence, test, metric, exception, and improvement. The emphasis is process engineering and auditability rather than checkbox compliance.

What I Built

Seven IAM workflows covering joiner-mover-leaver, access requests, provisioning, periodic reviews, privileged access, emergency revocation, and exceptions, supported by policies, standards, templates, fictional evidence, diagrams, testing, metrics, monitoring, and remediation planning.

Methods: NIST CSF 2.0 · NIST SP 800-53 · NIST SP 800-53A · IAM · GRC Engineering
