PROJECT 02 / AI GOVERNANCE

AI Governance in the Real World

A practical governance and risk program for AI-assisted routing and operational decision support, with human accountability at the center.

The Problem

A fictional logistics organization wants the benefits of AI-assisted dispatch and routing without unmanaged operational, privacy, security, fairness, safety, vendor, or reputational risk.

My Approach

I applied the NIST AI RMF lifecycle to define use context, risk ownership, decision rights, evaluation criteria, human review, monitoring, incident response, and evidence. RouteAssist remains decision support rather than an autonomous decision-maker.

What I Built

Use-case boundaries, system and stakeholder context, multi-domain risk assessment, governance controls, human review and override requirements, evaluation criteria, monitoring, incident response, evidence, and remediation structures.

Methods: NIST AI RMF · AI Governance · Human Oversight · Risk Management
