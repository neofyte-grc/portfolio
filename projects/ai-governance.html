PROJECT 02 / AI GOVERNANCE

AI Governance in the Real World

A governance and risk program for AI-assisted routing and operational decision support, with human accountability at the center.

The operating challenge

A fictional logistics organization wants to use AI-assisted dispatch and routing without creating unmanaged operational, privacy, security, fairness, safety, vendor, or reputational risk. The core governance question is where AI may assist and where accountable human decision-making must remain authoritative.

How I approached it

I used the NIST AI Risk Management Framework to structure governance around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. The work defines use context, ownership, decision rights, evaluation, human review, override, monitoring, incident response, vendor considerations, evidence, and remediation. RouteAssist is explicitly decision support—not an autonomous decision-maker.

The goal is not documentation for its own sake. The goal is traceable security work with clear ownership, practical execution, and evidence that supports review and improvement.

What the work demonstrates

The repository turns responsible-AI principles into an operating model: use-case boundaries, stakeholder and affected-party analysis, multi-domain risk assessment, governance controls, meaningful human oversight, evaluation criteria, monitoring, escalation, incident handling, evidence, and remediation structures.
