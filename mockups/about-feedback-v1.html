ABOUT TOMMY

Operations experience.
A security mindset.

PEOPLE. PROCESSES. TECHNOLOGY.

Nearly a decade in operations and logistics taught me to work where process, accountability, deadlines, vendors, documentation, and exceptions meet. That experience gave me a foundation in accountable processes, measurable performance, and disciplined follow-through.

I am deliberately applying that foundation to cybersecurity governance through hands-on, self-directed work in security risk, control mapping, IAM, cloud security, and AI governance.
