ABOUT TOMMY

Operations Experience.
A Security Mindset.

Security and the intersection
of people, processes, and technology.

My background is rooted in operations and logistics, where I learned that good systems only work when people can actually use them. Over 8+ years, I built experience navigating real workflows, competing priorities, process gaps, accountability, and the day-to-day realities that determine whether a procedure succeeds or fails.

Today, I bring that operational perspective into governance, risk, compliance, cloud security, and AI governance. My work focuses on turning frameworks and requirements into practical controls, repeatable processes, and security programs that make sense to the people responsible for carrying them out.

I approach security as an operating discipline—not a collection of documents. The goal is to understand the risk, design a workable process, communicate it clearly, and build enough structure that the right behavior becomes easier to repeat.
